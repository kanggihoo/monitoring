# Market Replay UI mockup prompts

Shared art direction: desktop Korean financial simulation game UI, 16:10, 1440px wide. Dense but calm professional interface. Pretendard-like Korean sans serif and tabular numerals. Light palette only: outer frame #DFE3E9, shell #E9ECF1, white cards, charcoal #14171A text, thin #E1E4E9 dividers, 12px card corners, extremely restrained soft shadows. Red #D0333F exclusively for rising prices and buy action; blue #2464CC exclusively for falling prices and sell action; no gradients, no neon, no glassmorphism, no candles. Line charts only. Korean copy should be clean and legible; prioritize layout over tiny text.

Market Replay home dashboard, header brand "MARKET REPLAY", game clock "2022. 04. 14 · 재생·거래" and compact countdown. Ticker of current news. Main column: a large market-movers board with tabs 전체 / 나스닥 / 국내 / 코인 and ranked horizontal red and blue performance bars. Right rail: season ranking top 5, live chat, position heatmap. Lower area: two-wallet account summary with KRW and USD, holdings, and a small timeline of market news. Include a quiet fee-free-time notice. Real app screenshot, not a presentation.
